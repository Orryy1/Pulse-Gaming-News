## Channel name and handle

- Name: **Pulse Gaming News**
- Handle: `@PulseGamingNews`

## About (description)

Gaming news, checked and explained. Pulse covers the stories that matter in under a minute: release dates, delays, prices, sales milestones, reveals and the industry moves behind them, with a clear read on what each one means and a source credit on every fact.

New Shorts every day at 12:00, 16:00 and 19:30 UK time, plus a breaking lane for the big ones.

Narration is a locally generated British synthetic voice. Visuals are official publisher material (Steam store art, official trailers, first-party newsroom images), credited in every description. Rumours are always labelled and never presented as fact.

Business: see the email on this page.

## Links (Customisation → Basic info → Links)

Add the channel's own newsletter or site first if one exists, then the Discord server if public. Nothing personal.

## Upload defaults (Settings → Upload defaults)

- Visibility: Public (the factory sets this per upload; leave the default Public once the first unlisted test looks right)
- Category: Gaming
- Language: English (United Kingdom)
- Comments: Allow all, hold potentially inappropriate for review
- Licence: Standard YouTube licence
- Altered or synthetic content: Yes (the factory sets this flag on every upload)
- Audience: not made for kids

## Pinned comment

The factory posts the first comment on every Short as the channel ("Our read: ... Sources are in the description."). The API cannot pin, so pin it from the YouTube app when the Discord publish notice arrives: open the video → Comments → ⋮ on the Pulse comment → Pin.

## Title and description house rules (enforced by the factory)

- Title: "[Game]: [the fact in 4-7 words]", under 60 characters, no exclamation marks, no clickbait words.
- Description: first line is the keyword lead ("Starfield delayed: ... Bethesda, Xbox news, checked by Pulse Gaming News."), then the body, sources, footage credit, the voice and visuals disclosure, the channel line and three hashtags (#Shorts #GamingNews #Game).
- Tags: the game, its franchise and platform terms, the story type, then evergreen terms, within 500 characters.
