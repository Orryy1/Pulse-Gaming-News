# Pulse Gaming calendar

Angles planned for a date. The editors see today's lines beside the live candidates and the news desk
sees the next three days. One entry a line: `YYYY-MM-DD lane: the angle`, lane being why, news,
evergreen, long, doc or any. Advice, not orders: the editor still judges the day's candidates and never
invents a story to fit the plan. Dates and claims in an angle are checked against the sources on the day.
