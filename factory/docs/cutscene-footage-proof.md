# Cutscene footage proof

The uncommitted candidate adds an opt-in cutscene source after Steam and official trailer footage. It searches metadata first, checks the selected upload again, downloads short sections, strips audio, screens the sections and finished clips, and records the YouTube ID, title and uploader. Clips share the existing publisher limits and have their own footage credit. The capture checklist omits explicitly labelled cutscenes and cinematic requests.

`CUTSCENE_FOOTAGE` defaults to `false`. `CUTSCENE_MAX_SECONDS` defaults to 20 seconds for Shorts. Long videos scale the video-wide allowance from one to six times that amount. Existing publisher limits, including Rockstar's 10 seconds and Nintendo's stills-only rule, still apply. The source requires an actual heavy PC reservation on Windows.
