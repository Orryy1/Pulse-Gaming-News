# GTA VI: things to test on stream (12 Nov 2026)

Every claim below comes from pre-launch previews (Edge, Game Informer, IGN and others) as gathered by GTABase, GTA BOOM and GamesRadar, or from fan myths. None are proven in the real game yet. That is the point: each one is a "previews said X, I tried it" clip. Systems only, no story spoilers. Say the claim, try it, show the result.

Order: biggest viral question first, and things you can do early in the game first (the open world opens up after the first missions).

## Myths to settle (clips that start arguments)

- **"There's a hidden third playable character" (the three birds on the cover art).** Look for any switch beyond Jason and Lucia. Easy to disprove, big comment bait. Say "previews say two" first.
- **"No first-person mode."** Previews say none, except when aiming some weapons. Check the settings menu on day 1.
- **"1440p at 30 fps."** An unofficial preview figure, not confirmed by PlayStation or Rockstar. Show the settings menu and any performance mode on PS5 or PS5 Pro.
- **"Police don't show as dots on the minimap."** Check in your first chase.
- **"The radio has a copyright-free toggle for creators."** Check the audio settings on day 1. This matters for your VODs and for Pulse clips, so it is worth confirming first.
- **"80-hour story."** Don't test this on stream for weeks (spoiler window). Track your hours and only mention the total later.
