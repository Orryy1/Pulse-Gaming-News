# GTA VI before you play: confirmed facts (planned 14 Nov 2026 for the 17 Nov long video)

Primary sources only: Rockstar Newswire, Rockstar Support, Rockstar's own GTA VI site, Take-Two, the platform stores and Rockstar's official trailers. Never cite Wikipedia on air. No leaks, datamines or insider claims, ever. Recheck anything time-sensitive on the day.

## Confirmed

### Release and unlock times

- Release: Thursday November 19, 2026 on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. It unlocks at midnight local time in each region, New Zealand first at 11:00 UTC on November 18 (Rockstar Support, 3 Nov 2026): https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/29jV3Cv2cCgvwM1XgtMO5o
- Say it as: out from November 18 in New Zealand, then midnight local time everywhere else on November 19. In the US that is midnight Eastern on November 19 (05:00 UTC); in the UK, midnight on November 19.
- Pre-load opened November 10 (PS5 at midnight local, Xbox at 00:00 GMT); the download is up to 212 GB (Rockstar Newswire, 3 Nov 2026): https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/k4ak9okk485712/grand-theft-auto-vi-pre-load-begins-on-november-10
- Boxed copies hold a download code, not a disc, and are in shops from November 12 (same Newswire post; Rockstar Store): https://store.rockstargames.com/game/buy-gta-vi

### Price and editions (US dollars first)

- Standard Edition $79.99, Ultimate Edition $99.99 (Take-Two): https://www.take2games.com/ir/news/rockstar-games-announces-pre-orders-grand-theft-auto-vi
- UK: £69.99 Standard, £89.99 Ultimate (Rockstar Store UK listing).
- Eurozone: €79.99 Standard, €99.99 Ultimate (European store listings at pre-order launch; give as the store price, recheck on the day).
- Ultimate adds premium vehicles, weapons, apparel and more for the story, as Rockstar lists it (Rockstar Newswire, pre-orders): https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/5171972o3ak5oa/pre-order-grand-theft-auto-vi-on-june-25
- Pre-order bonus: the Vintage Vice City Pack for orders placed before November 20; digital pre-orders also get a free month of GTA+ (same Newswire post).
- Rockstar describes GTA VI as a single player experience in its pre-order post; no new online mode has been announced.

### Platforms

- PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S only at launch.
- PlayStation's page lists PS5 Pro Enhanced, DualSense haptics and Tempest 3D Audio; frame rate, resolution and graphics modes are not announced there: https://www.playstation.com/en-gb/games/grand-theft-auto-vi/
- PC: not announced by Rockstar or Take-Two.

### The two leads

- Rockstar's own site introduces the two leads as Lucia Caminos and Jason Duval: https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/only-in-leonida
- Setup only, as Rockstar's site gives it: Lucia is recently out of prison and set on building the good life her mother wanted; Jason is a former soldier raised among grifters, now working in the Keys.
- The same page names the people around them: Cal Hampton, Boobie Ike, Dre'Quan Priest, Real Dimez (Bae-Luxe and Roxy), Raul Bautista and Brian Heder. Describe them only as the page does; nothing about where their stories go.
- Switching between Jason and Lucia: Rockstar's studio head said in interviews that it depends on story progress, some missions lock you to one of them, and they can work as a pair or apart. Say "said" and name the interview as the press reports it.

### The setting

- GTA VI is set in the state of Leonida, Rockstar's take on Florida, and returns to Vice City (Rockstar's GTA VI site): https://www.rockstargames.com/VI
- Rockstar's site names six regions: Vice City, the Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga National Park: https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/only-in-leonida
- Map size: Rockstar's Aaron Garbut told Game Informer (issue 382, 29 Sep 2026) the map is about twice the size of GTA 5's. Rockstar has given no square-kilometre figure; any such number is a fan estimate.
- Rockstar's printable cover art poster and map of Leonida are a free digital download on November 19 on the official site (Rockstar Newswire, 3 Nov 2026, link above).

- Trailer 1 (December 2023) and Trailer 2 (May 2025) on Rockstar's channel; the Extended Look (27 Aug 2026, 26:48, in-game on PS5): https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/4k138k8okkk483/grand-theft-auto-vi-an-extended-look-now-playing

- GTA VI: The Album, 34 tracks, from Atlantic Records on November 19: https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/7599a881942544/announcing-grand-theft-auto-vi-the-album-coming-november-19

## Reported (say "reportedly" and who reported it; never as fact)

- PC: GamesBeat reported (13 Oct 2026, four anonymous sources) a PC version planned for early 2027. Rockstar did not comment.
- Frame rate: press reports say 30 frames per second on consoles at launch, with no performance mode promised. Rockstar has not announced a frame rate.
- Square-kilometre map sizes online are fan estimates, not Rockstar figures.
