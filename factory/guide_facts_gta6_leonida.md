# GTA VI's Leonida from Rockstar's own trailers and site: confirmed facts (planned 14 Nov 2026 for the 18 Nov long video)

Primary sources only: Rockstar's GTA VI site, Rockstar Newswire, Rockstar's official trailers and screenshots, and Rockstar's people quoted by name. Never cite Wikipedia on air. No leaks, datamines, leaked coordinates or fan-made maps as fact. Rockstar has not published a full readable map before launch: say where a place sits only when Rockstar's own words or footage show it.

## Confirmed

### The state

- GTA VI is set in Leonida, Rockstar's take on Florida, and returns to Vice City: https://www.rockstargames.com/VI
- Rockstar's site names six regions: Vice City, the Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga National Park: https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/only-in-leonida
- Size: Rockstar's Aaron Garbut told Game Informer (issue 382, 29 Sep 2026) the map is about twice the size of GTA 5's; outlets also report Rockstar comparing it with three times Red Dead Redemption 2. Rockstar has given no square-kilometre figure.
- Rockstar has said you won't see all of it on story missions alone (as reported from its interviews; name the outlet).
- Wildlife and weather: more than 170 animal species, and hurricanes and tropical storms (Rockstar, Game Informer issue 382).
- Rockstar's printable cover art poster and map of Leonida are a free digital download on November 19 on the official site (Rockstar Newswire, 3 Nov 2026): https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/k4ak9okk485712/grand-theft-auto-vi-pre-load-begins-on-november-10

### The six regions: what Rockstar itself says (checked on rockstargames.com/VI, 14 Nov 2026)

- Rockstar's GTA VI site sums up Vice City as the darkest side of the sunniest place in America, and its people pages call it a city of neon-lit streets, where the local legend Boobie Ike built a business empire that includes property and a recording studio: https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/only-in-leonida/vice-city
- The Leonida Keys are where Jason has been working and where Brian Heder runs a boat yard (Rockstar's people pages): https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/only-in-leonida/leonida-keys
- Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga National Park are named on Rockstar's site with their own explore pages, but Rockstar has said little else about them in text. Describe them only by their names and by what Rockstar's own footage and screenshots visibly show; never invent what is there.
- Lucia: recently out of prison, set on building the good life her mother wanted. Jason: a former soldier raised among grifters. Setup only, as Rockstar's site gives it; nothing about where their story goes.
