## The week's biggest fault

Only one long video went out in seven days (planned: five long rankings or guides, a documentary and a round-up). Causes, all found and fixed by 27 Nov morning:

1. A Short stuck in production held the Mac "busy" for long renders indefinitely (fixed 00:15, 27 Nov).
2. The factory was restarted by 27 deploys in 31 hours, and each restart killed a long build in progress (DEPLOY_WAIT_LONG, 07:00, 27 Nov: deploys now wait for a long build).
3. A fresh long video's preparation (depth, 3D clips, graphics pre-check) took 2 h 40 min and used up the gap before the render itself. It is kept once done, so the Cyberpunk documentary now only needs a 2-hour gap (the next is about 15:00 to 17:15 UK today).
