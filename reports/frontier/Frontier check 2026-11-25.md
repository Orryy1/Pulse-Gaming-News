## The three biggest gaps (now in the queue as items 46 to 48)

1. **The Pulse Check "My verdict:" line and other machine tells.** Sounds human is the widest gap of any dimension (2.9 against 6.6), and it links to average viewed (0.25). "My verdict:" closes 14 of 31 Shorts because the friend pass and clarity rewrite require it (friendpass.ts:523, clarity.ts:308). Judges named it in about half their notes. Build: ENDING_FORMS, off by default, 50/50 by episode; arm B drops the spoken "My verdict:" (the card can stay) and lets the writer end on a callback, consequence, punchline or plain fact, with no closing shape used more than twice a day. Pass: blind sounds-human mark at least 1.0 higher in arm B and average viewed no more than 2 points lower, 20 Shorts a side.
2. **Non-stories and shopping lists chosen as Shorts.** Seven of the ten lowest-marked Shorts were GTA 6 utility or "nothing known" stories (presets unconfirmed, New Zealand switch unconfirmed, bonus cutoff, preorder price, track list, patch notes, outage reports). An "unconfirmed" story with a real conflict ("Rockstar layoffs remain a prediction") scored best, so the test is conflict or surprise, not the word. Payoff links to views (0.24), one point to average viewed (0.33). Build: STORY_PREMISE_GATE, off by default, 50/50 by episode; before writing, a cheap check drops a candidate whose whole point is that nothing is known, a price or edition list, or patch notes, unless it carries a conflict or a surprise, and takes the next candidate. Pass: engaged rate 5 points higher in arm B with median views no lower, 20 a side.
3. **A held opening shot and broken captions in one shared layout.** 4 of 6 sheets hold one shot for 5 to 8 seconds behind the title; captions break mid-phrase on 6 of 6; 1 to 3 reused shots per Short. The 16 Nov retention lesson already found a still picture costs about 24 points of a Short's audience. Build: OPEN_MOTION plus CAPTION_PHRASES, off by default, 50/50 by episode: first cut by 2.5 s with the title over moving footage, captions split only at phrase breaks, no shot reused. Pass: average viewed 3 points higher in arm B, 20 a side. claude/gap-untemplate (SHORT_SOUND_PLAN, SHORT_LAYOUTS) is the natural home.

Already running and not repeated: the writers' room (SCRIPT_ROOM_SHARE=0.5). This week's blind result backs it; it should be judged as soon as it reaches 20 a side.
