## Intent

Episode one of Pulse Gaming's seven-part **System Trace** series. Start with the
familiar feeling that a game can report a high frame rate yet still look jerky,
then reveal frame pacing as the hidden rhythm behind perceived smoothness. The
piece should feel like a forensic system readout: precise, energetic and easy to
grasp on a phone.

## Customizations

- Original animated timing diagrams and kinetic typography; no third-party
  gameplay, screenshots, logos or captured footage.
- Pulse Gaming amber, near-black and warm-white palette applied to a bold,
  high-contrast editorial system.
- Local British male synthetic narration, exact word-timed captions and a
  restrained rights-cleared music bed mixed below the voice.
- A consistent **SYSTEM TRACE // 01** episode marker, with the teaching layouts
  varied enough that the series does not read as seven template swaps.

## Notes
