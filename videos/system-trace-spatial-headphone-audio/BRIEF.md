## Thesis

Spatial headphone audio filters each positioned sound with direction-dependent
head and ear cues before delivering the result through two channels.

## Visual system

curves and a rotating sound field. Near-black, Pulse amber, warm white and
proof cyan. No gameplay, screenshots, platform marks or borrowed diagrams.
